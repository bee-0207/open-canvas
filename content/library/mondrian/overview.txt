This is Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow, by Piet Mondrian, painted in 1930. It is square. Thick, straight black lines divide it into rectangles. A large red square fills most of the upper right. A blue rectangle sits in the bottom left corner, and a small yellow one in the bottom right corner. The other blocks are white. Each color plays its own instrument, and you will feel a buzz every time you cross a black line. Touch anywhere to explore. To hear about a spot, rest your finger on it and keep still.
