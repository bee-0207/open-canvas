This is The Great Wave off Kanagawa, a Japanese woodblock print by Katsushika Hokusai, made around 1831. It is wider than it is tall. A giant blue wave rises on the left and curls over toward the right, its white crest breaking into claws of foam. Three long boats with rowers are caught in the waves. Far away in the center, small and snow-capped, sits Mount Fuji, under the curl of the wave. The top half is a pale beige sky. Touch anywhere to explore. To hear about a spot, rest your finger on it and keep still.
