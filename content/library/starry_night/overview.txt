This is The Starry Night, by Vincent van Gogh, painted in 1889. It is a little wider than it is tall. Most of the painting is a deep blue night sky full of swirling brushstrokes, with a great spiral turning in the middle. Eleven glowing stars are scattered across it, and a bright crescent moon shines in the top right corner. Below, dark blue hills roll behind a small village with a church steeple. On the left, a tall dark cypress tree rises like a flame from the bottom almost to the top. Touch anywhere to explore. To hear about a spot, rest your finger on it and keep still.
